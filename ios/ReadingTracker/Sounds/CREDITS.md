# Focus sound recordings

All recordings are public domain or CC0, from Wikimedia Commons. They can be used in a paid app with no credit
required; they're listed here so their origin is on record. Each was trimmed to its steadiest stretch, made into a
seamless loop (end crossfaded into start), widened to stereo where the original was mono, and loudness-matched.

| File | Source | Licence | Recorded by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rain.m4a` | [Rain (1).ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_(1).ogg) | Public domain | ezwa |
| `fireplace.m4a` | [Dry grass burning in open fireplace.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dry_grass_burning_in_open_fireplace.ogg), with a soft low roar added | Public domain | ezwa |

White noise is generated by the app. Café and Beach use the app's generated sounds until their recordings are added.
