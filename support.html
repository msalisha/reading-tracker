Help and support

Reading Tracker for iPhone

How does the free trial work?

Tap Start my 3-day free trial when you first open the app. Everything is unlocked for 3 days. After that, one payment unlocks it forever. Nothing is charged automatically, and there's no subscription.

What happens when the trial ends?

Everything you've logged stays visible, and you can still save a backup. To add books or log reading again, tap Unlock forever.

I bought it on another iPhone

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I move my reading to a new phone?

On the old phone, Settings → Save a backup, then send the file to yourself (AirDrop, Files or email). On the new phone, Settings → Import a backup. Backups from the web Reading Tracker import the same way.

The barcode scan doesn't find my book

Some books, especially new or local editions, aren't in the public catalogues. Type the title, author and page count yourself. The rest of the app works the same.

The timer isn't on my lock screen

Check iPhone Settings → Reading Tracker → Live Activities is on.

Still stuck?

Open an issue at github.com/msalisha/reading-tracker/issues and we'll get back to you.
