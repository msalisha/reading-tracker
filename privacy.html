Privacy policy

Reading Tracker for iPhone · last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

Your reading stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, store or sell any of your data, and the app has no accounts, ads or analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Your books, reading sessions, notes, quotes and ratings are saved only on your device.
  • Backups you export are files you choose where to put. We never see them.
  • Widgets and the lock-screen timer read the same on-device data.

What leaves your phone

  • Book lookups. When you scan a barcode or tap Look up, the app sends only the book's ISBN to Open Library (openlibrary.org) and, if needed, Google Books (googleapis.com) to fetch the title, author, page count and cover. No personal information is sent with it. Those services' own privacy policies apply to their sites.
  • Purchases. The free trial and the unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment or personal details.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a barcode or a quote. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
