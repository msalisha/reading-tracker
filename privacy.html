Privacy policy

Pageturner for iPhone · last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Your reading stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. We don't collect, store or sell any of your data, and the app has no accounts, ads or analytics.

Where your data is kept

  • Your books, reading sessions, notes, quotes, ratings and covers are saved on your device.
  • iCloud sync. If you're signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy in your own private iCloud storage so your library stays in sync across your iPhone and iPad and is safe if you change phones. This storage belongs to your Apple Account: we can't see or access it. You can turn it off for Pageturner in the Settings app (tap your name, then iCloud).
  • Backups you export are files you choose where to put. We never see them.
  • Goodreads import. The Goodreads file you choose is read on your device. Nothing is sent to Goodreads, and we never see it.
  • Widgets and the lock-screen timer read the same on-device data.

What leaves your phone

  • Book lookups. When you scan a barcode or tap Look up, the app sends only the book's ISBN to Open Library (openlibrary.org) and, if needed, Google Books (googleapis.com) to fetch the title, author, page count and cover. No personal information is sent with it. Those services' own privacy policies apply to their sites.
  • Finding a cover on Google. If you choose Search Google Images, Google's own page opens inside the app with the book's title and author (or its ISBN) as the search. That page is Google's, and Google's privacy policy applies to it. Pageturner only saves the picture you pick, on your phone.
  • Purchases. The free trial and the unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment or personal details.

Camera

The camera is used only while you scan a barcode or a quote. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
